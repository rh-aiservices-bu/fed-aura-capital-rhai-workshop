Fed Aura Capital
Mortgage Product Catalog, Rev. 2026-Q3

PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Fed Aura Capital offers five mortgage products designed for different borrower
profiles. All rates are as of September 2026 and subject to change based on
market conditions and borrower qualification.

30-YEAR FIXED RATE MORTGAGE

Rate: 6.25% APR
Term: 30 years
Minimum credit score: 620
Minimum down payment: 3% (with PMI required below 20%)
Maximum loan amount: $766,550 (conforming limit)
Best for: Borrowers who want predictable monthly payments and plan to stay in
their home long-term. The lower monthly payment compared to shorter terms makes
this the most popular choice for first-time buyers.

15-YEAR FIXED RATE MORTGAGE

Rate: 5.75% APR
Term: 15 years
Minimum credit score: 640
Minimum down payment: 5%
Maximum loan amount: $766,550 (conforming limit)
Best for: Borrowers who can afford higher monthly payments and want to build
equity faster. Total interest paid over the life of the loan is significantly
lower than the 30-year option.

FHA LOAN

Rate: 6.50% APR
Term: 30 years
Minimum credit score: 580 (500-579 with 10% down payment)
Minimum down payment: 3.5%
Maximum loan amount: Varies by county (see FHA loan limits)
Mortgage insurance: Required for the life of the loan (MIP)
Best for: First-time homebuyers or borrowers with lower credit scores who may
not qualify for conventional financing. More flexible qualification requirements
but requires mortgage insurance regardless of down payment.

VA LOAN

Rate: 5.95% APR
Term: 15 or 30 years
Minimum credit score: No minimum required by VA (Fed Aura Capital requires 580)
Minimum down payment: 0%
Maximum loan amount: No limit for eligible veterans with full entitlement
Funding fee: 1.25% to 3.3% (may be financed into the loan)
Best for: Eligible active-duty service members, veterans, and surviving spouses.
No down payment required and no private mortgage insurance.

JUMBO LOAN

Rate: 6.75% APR
Term: 15 or 30 years
Minimum credit score: 700
Minimum down payment: 10%
Minimum loan amount: $766,551
Maximum loan amount: $3,000,000
Cash reserves required: 12 months of mortgage payments
Best for: Borrowers purchasing high-value properties that exceed conforming loan
limits. Stricter qualification requirements reflect the higher risk profile.

RATE LOCK POLICY

Borrowers may lock their rate for 30, 45, or 60 days at no additional cost.
Extended locks of 90 days are available for a 0.125% fee. Rate locks are
available after the loan application is submitted and the initial credit pull
is complete.

CLOSING COST ESTIMATE

Typical closing costs range from 2% to 5% of the loan amount and include
origination fees, appraisal, title insurance, and recording fees. Fed Aura
Capital offers a no-closing-cost option where closing costs are rolled into
the loan balance at a slightly higher interest rate (typically 0.25% higher).

DOWN PAYMENT ASSISTANCE

Fed Aura Capital participates in state and local down payment assistance
programs. Eligible borrowers may qualify for grants or forgivable second
mortgages to cover part or all of the down payment. Ask your loan officer
about available programs in your area.
